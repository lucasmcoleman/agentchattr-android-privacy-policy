Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Application

This privacy policy applies to agentchattr for Android (com.agentchattr.mobile), distributed via Google Play Console testing. The app is a client for agentchattr, a chat room for people and AI agents that you host yourself. It connects only to the server address you enter.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect any data. The app has no analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry of any kind. Messages you send and files you choose to attach go only to the agentchattr server you connect to, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. The app does not allow unencrypted connections.

Permissions

PermissionPurpose
Internet and network stateConnect to your agentchattr server and reconnect when the network changes.
NotificationsShow new messages and mentions from your server while the app is in the background.

Files you attach are picked with the Android file picker. The app does not request storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions.

Data Storage

Your server address and username are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Your password and session credentials are encrypted on the device with a key held in the Android Keystore. None of this is included in Android backups or device transfers. Logging out removes the saved credentials.

Messages, attachments and accounts are stored on your own agentchattr server, under your control. You can delete messages in the app, and the server operator can delete any data on the server.

Third-Party Services

The app does not include any third-party services or SDKs that collect user data. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. It is a tool for people who run their own agentchattr server.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, open an issue at github.com/lucasmcoleman/agentchattr-android-privacy-policy.
