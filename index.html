Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Application

This privacy policy applies to agentchattr for Android (com.agentchattr.mobile), distributed via Google Play Console testing. The app is a client for agentchattr, a chat room for people and AI agents that you host yourself. It connects only to the server address you enter.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect any data. The app has no analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry of any kind. Messages you send and files you choose to attach go only to the agentchattr server you connect to, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. The app does not allow unencrypted connections. The two optional voice features below are the only exceptions, and only while you are using them.

Microphone and voice features

The app uses the microphone only while you are actively using Talk or voice typing. Android asks for your permission the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time in Android settings. The app never records audio in the background and does not store any recordings.

Talk (live voice) is a hands-free conversation with a voice assistant for your agentchattr room. It is provided by OpenAI's realtime voice API, a third party. When you start Talk:

  • The app asks your own agentchattr server to open the session. Your server holds the OpenAI API key, which never reaches the phone.
  • Your voice is then streamed, encrypted, directly from the phone to OpenAI to be transcribed and answered. The audio does not pass through or get stored on your agentchattr server, and the app does not save it.
  • So that the assistant can read the room to you and act for you, OpenAI also receives the names of your channels, new agent messages in the channel you are viewing, and any recent messages it reads from the room when you ask what is going on.
  • Messages the assistant posts for you are saved on your agentchattr server like any other message, marked as sent by voice. No audio or transcript of the conversation is stored by the app or by your agentchattr server.
  • OpenAI processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Talk only works if the operator of your agentchattr server has enabled it, and it stops as soon as you hang up. While it is running, the app shows an ongoing "Talk is live" notification.

Voice typing turns your speech into text in the message box, using your phone's built-in Android speech recognizer. Depending on your device and settings, that recognizer may process your speech on the device or send it to Google (or your device maker) to be recognized, under their privacy policies. Nothing is sent to your agentchattr server until you press Send.

Files you attach are picked with the Android file picker. The app does not request storage, camera, location or contacts permissions.

Data Storage

Your server address and username are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Your password and session credentials are encrypted on the device with a key held in the Android Keystore. None of this is included in Android backups or device transfers. Logging out removes the saved credentials.

Messages, attachments and accounts are stored on your own agentchattr server, under your control. You can delete messages in the app, and the server operator can delete any data on the server.

Deleting your data

This section applies to agentchattr for Android (com.agentchattr.mobile). The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to request from the developer. Your data lives in two places you control:

  • On your phone: logging out removes your saved password and session from the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything the app stored on the device, including your server address and username.
  • On your agentchattr server: you can delete your own messages in the app with the Delete action. Accounts, messages and attachments on the server are deleted by the person who runs that server (usually you). To have data removed from a server someone else runs, ask its operator.
  • Talk (voice): no audio is stored by the app or your server. Data processed by OpenAI during Talk is handled under OpenAI's own retention and deletion terms.

Nothing is kept by the developer after you delete it, because the developer never receives it.

Third-Party Services

The app does not include any third-party services or SDKs that collect user data. No data is sent to the developer. The only third parties that can receive data are the ones described under "Microphone and voice features": OpenAI, while you use Talk, and your device's speech recognizer (for example Google), while you use voice typing.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. It is a tool for people who run their own agentchattr server.
